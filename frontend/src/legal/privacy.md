## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (accounts, categories, tags, transactions, notes, and settings such as your currency and theme)
is stored in your browser's storage on your device. It stays there until you delete it, for example by clearing the
site's data in your browser.

## Google account and Google Sheets

Syncing is optional. If you connect a Google account, the app asks for these permissions:

- **See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files used with this app** (drive.file). The app
  uses it to create one spreadsheet named “{{appName}}” in your Drive and to read and update it. It cannot see any
  other file in your Drive.
- **Your email address** (openid, email), shown in Settings so you know which account is connected.

Your data travels directly between your device and Google. The spreadsheet belongs to you: you can open, edit, share
or delete it in Google Drive at any time, and Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies
to it.

{{appName}}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the sync feature you turned on. We don't
transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or let humans read it, except with your explicit permission, for
security reasons, or where the law requires.

## Staying signed in

To keep you signed in, a small sign-in service run by us exchanges your Google sign-in for a refresh token. The token
is encrypted and stored only in a cookie on your device, which page scripts can't read. The service keeps no copy and
no database of users; it uses the token only to get new short-lived access tokens for your device. If the service is
unavailable, the app asks you to sign in again instead.

Like any website, our hosting provider processes technical information such as IP addresses and request times to
deliver the app and keep it secure. We don't use this to identify or profile you.

## Cookies and browser storage

- The sign-in cookie described above, only if you connect a Google account.
- Browser storage for your data, settings, and a short-lived Google access token.

There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

## Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your information. Google processes it as described above because you chose to sync with
your Google account.

## Keeping or deleting your data

- **Disconnect** in Settings stops syncing on that device, signs you out and revokes the app's Google access.
- You can also remove the app's access in your [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
- Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes everything stored on that device.
- Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive until you delete it there.

## Security

The app is served over HTTPS, the sign-in cookie is encrypted, and the app can only access the spreadsheet it
created. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device and Google account protected.
